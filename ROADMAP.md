# WarbandTogether roadmap

How far coop is from Warband's single-player campaign, feature by feature, following a career from adventurer to sovereign.

| Milestone | Done |
|---|---|
| Adventurer | 23 / 113 |
| Mercenary & vassal | 1 / 18 |
| Lord | 0 / 8 |
| Sovereign | 0 / 2 |
| Full parity | 10 / 30 |

## Mercenary & vassal

Take contracts, follow marshal campaigns, swear fealty, receive a fief, do kingdom quests.

| Feature | Area | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assaulting a castle or town | Combat | Works |  |
| Lords defending a castle in a local siege | Combat | Planned | In a siege you lead yourself, lords stationed in the castle do not take part in the defense. |
| Garrison losses counted as yours after a local siege | Combat | Known issue | After a siege you lead yourself, you can lose troops of the same type as the defenders. |
| Besieging: siege camp, ladders, towers and parley | Combat | Planned | Sieges start straight away: there is no siege camp, no ladders or towers to build and no talks with the defenders. |
| Defending a besieged castle or town | Combat | Planned | You cannot help defend a castle or town under siege. |
| Claiming a conquered castle or town | Combat | Planned | Captured castles and towns go to a shared player faction; you cannot claim them for yourself yet. |
| Raiding villages | Combat | Planned | You cannot raid villages yet. |
| Sneaking into enemy towns | Towns | Planned | Sneaking into enemy towns is not available yet. |
| Lord quests | Quests | Planned | Lords don't give quests yet. |
| Army and kingdom quests | Quests | Planned | Army and kingdom quests aren't in yet; they need joining a kingdom first. |
| Lords reacting to each player | Lords, ladies & companions | Planned | Lords do not yet act on how much they like you: they will not ride to your aid or invite you to feasts. |
| Mercenary contracts | Politics & kingdoms | Known issue | Lords offer mercenary contracts, but accepting one has no lasting effect yet. |
| Serving a kingdom (per-player faction) | Politics & kingdoms | Planned | Players cannot join a kingdom yet; everyone plays as an independent. |
| Swearing fealty | Politics & kingdoms | Known issue | You can swear an oath to a king, but it is forgotten when you leave the hall. |
| Marshals and kingdom campaigns | Politics & kingdoms | Planned | Kingdom campaigns run without you: marshals never call on players yet. |
| Wars, peace and lord politics | Politics & kingdoms | Partly works | Wars and peace run for the whole world, but lords held in castle prisons never escape yet. |
| News of wars, peace, sieges and raids | Reports & screens | Planned | You are not told about world events such as wars, sieges and raids. |
| Battles between players of enemy kingdoms | Coop features | Planned | Players on opposite sides of a war cannot fight each other yet. |
